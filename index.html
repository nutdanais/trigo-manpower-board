Roles & permissions

What each role may see and change. Edit also grants view. Changes apply to everyone with that role straight away — including anyone with the app open right now. Admin always keeps full access, so nobody can lock themselves out of this screen.

Hiding an Overview section keeps it off the page and skips its query. It is not a data barrier: those sections are worked out from the same mission and assignment records the board itself needs, so treat it as tidying the dashboard, not as protecting a secret.
