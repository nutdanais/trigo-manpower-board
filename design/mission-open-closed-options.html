Open and closed missions in the Missions table

You can't quickly see or filter open against closed missions. I read the Missions tab code and ran it on the demo data. The short answer: don't add a column. Make the Status column's Closed value unmistakable, and make the Open and Closed counts at the top of the tab the filter.

What the tab does today

  1. Closed missions are hidden on arrival. The tab opens on Open, so a closed mission does not exist on screen until someone finds the small Open / Closed / All switch between the Board picker and Filters. It carries no counts.

  2. The Open and Closed tiles are display only. I clicked the Closed tile on the demo and the list did not change. They are the first place the eye goes to ask "how many are closed?", and they do nothing.

  3. "Open" is never written in a row. An open mission says On board, Hidden, Off the board or Not started. Only a closed one says Closed 20 Oct 2026. So the Status column already is the open / closed column; it just never names the open side.

  4. Closed looks like "parked". On the demo's All view, Hidden, Not started and Closed are the same grey pill with a hollow dot. A finished job reads the same as a paused one.

  5. Filters → Board status has no Closed. It lists On board, Hidden, Off the board and Not started. That is deliberate (the switch already scopes the list, so Open plus Closed would show nothing), but it means the only way to reach Closed is the small switch.

Three ways to fix it

Not recommended

A. A new Open / Closed column

  • Repeats what Status already says.
  • Breaks the list-tab rule of one status pill per row (handover-list-tabs, section 3).
  • The table already shows eight columns by default, and a ninth costs width on every row.
  • It would still not filter anything.
Half the answer

B. Status column only

  • Make the Closed pill solid and darker, so it is no longer confused with Hidden or Not started.
  • Cheap and safe, and it fixes the reading problem.
  • It leaves the filtering problem as it is.
Recommended

C. B, plus the counts as the filter

  • The Open / Closed / All switch shows its counts.
  • The Open and Closed tiles filter when clicked, and the active one is underlined.
  • No separate chip row. Chips in this app mean a category (board, service area); a third row under the tiles next to By area would add clutter for one yes/no choice.

What the build touches

Closed pillA new status kind in statusPillHtml and the lt-st styles. The kind keeps the existing grey family; the dot is filled.
Counts on the switchSet from renderMissionStats, which already counts open and closed for the chosen board. The words stay translatable in i18n-th.js; the numbers are added after translation.
Clickable tilesOpen and Closed tiles become buttons that set state.missions.status, the same value the switch sets. Total stays plain text.
Phone cardThe key labels in renderPhoneViewCard.
Tests and demoExtend tests/e2e/missions-tab.e2e.js. The demo already has a closed mission (Chonburi 209), so /?demo shows the change with no new data and no database change.

The demo proves the look and the click behaviour. It does not prove the real database, row-level security, live updates between people, or real data.

Decisions for you

  1. Go with C (no column, solid Closed pill, counts on the switch, clickable tiles)? Or only B?

  2. Should the tab open on Open as now, or on All with closed rows sorted last? I would keep Open: that is the working list, and Closed is one click away.

  3. Should Manpower's and Host's tiles also become filters, for consistency? I only tested the Missions tab; I did not check whether they respond to a click.
