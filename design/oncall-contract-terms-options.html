On-call contract terms: Daily or Monthly

On-call inspectors are hired on one of two terms. This page shows how the app could record the term and show it on the employee card, the profile and the Manpower List. Three ways to mark the card are shown below, A to C. The form, profile and list work the same whichever card option you pick.

Language All names are invented sample data

What the app does today

Two contract typesEach person is permanent or oncall. The database allows only these two values.
On-call is used everywhereAbout 40 places check for on-call: the Available On-call pool, the On-call free count, utilization, the Overview charts and the Excel export. They should keep working unchanged.
The cardOn-call cards have a dashed warm border and an OC chip where permanent cards show the position. On-call people have no TRIGO ID, so the corner where the ID sits is empty.
Proposed recordA new field, On-call terms (Daily or Monthly), used only when the contract type is On-call. It is a separate field, so the 40 on-call checks stay as they are. Existing on-call people start as Not set until someone fills it in. Bulk edit can fill many at once.

ATerm tag in the card's corner

Recommended

The word Daily or Monthly sits in the top-right corner, where permanent cards show the TRIGO ID. Monthly is filled and Daily is outlined, so they can be told apart at a glance.

Good
  • The full word is readable, with no code to learn.
  • It uses empty space, so the card keeps its size and the bottom row is unchanged.
  • A card with no tag shows clearly that the term is not set yet.
Watch
  • If on-call people get TRIGO IDs later, the ID and the tag share that corner and a long first name gets shorter.
  • In Thai, รายเดือน is a little wider than Monthly.

BThe OC chip shows the term

The OC chip gets a second part: OC | D for Daily and OC | M for Monthly. Monthly fills the chip. Where the term is not set, the chip is the plain OC it is today.

Good
  • The term stays next to the OC chip, where people already look for the contract type.
  • The top corner stays free for a TRIGO ID if on-call people get one later.
Watch
  • D and M are codes people have to learn. The full word is only in the tooltip.
  • The chip is wider, so a long area name such as Wellgrow is cut short sooner.

CThe border shows the term

Monthly on-call cards get a solid dark-warm border. Daily cards keep today's dashed border. The pool header has a small key.

Good
  • No extra text on the card, so it is the calmest of the three.
  • Monthly people stand out in the pool when you pick who to call in.
Watch
  • People need the key to know what the border means.
  • Not set looks the same as Daily.
  • The selected-card outline is also a solid frame, so the two could be confused.

Questions before building

  1. Which card option: A, B or C? I recommend A.
  2. Is it only shown, or does it change any numbers? I've assumed it is only shown. For example, it does not change utilization or the On-call free count. If Monthly people are paid to be available, you may want Not called in counted differently for them.
  3. Should the Available On-call pool list Monthly first? Today it is sorted by name.
  4. Thai wording. รายวัน / รายเดือน, as used on the cards in Thai mode above?
  5. Does the term change over time? If people move from Daily to Monthly and past reports must show the old term, the app needs a dated history, not one field. One field is simpler and is what is shown here.
