Manpower Board · Overview tab review

Overview: four ways forward

A review of the Overview dashboard as it stands on the branch claude/amazing-ritchie-ukwily, then four design directions to choose from. Every mockup uses the same sample day so you can compare like with like: 40 staff on 2 boards, 9 deployed, 2 on leave, 20 permanent idle, 9 on-call free, 4 missions.

What changed in the code

Shipped

Board selector

First row of the tab, above "Needs attention today": All boards plus one pill per board with its headcount. It scopes every section, History included, and the device remembers it.

Shipped

Summary → detail → history

History and Engineer workload moved from the middle of the page to the end, under an Over time heading. The day's cards sit under In detail: Day/night next to By engineer, Leave next to Host coverage, then By service area.

Shipped

Past days stay put

Adding an employee was already handled for headcount. Three gaps are now closed: Host coverage risk counts only deployments up to that date, By engineer uses the same roster rule, and someone deactivated today stays in every earlier day's History.

Review

What still gets in the way

Found by walking the tab at desktop (1400px) and phone (390px) widths. Severity means how often it costs a user time, not how hard it is to fix.

F1 · status bar + Action queueHigh

The same alert is said twice

"20 permanent staff idle" heads the status bar, and "20 permanent staff not assigned yet" heads the Action queue right below it. The reader parses two boxes to learn one fact.

F2 · status bar, KPI, Day/nightHigh

"Day 3" and "Day 8" on one page

The status bar's Day / Night chips count missions (3 / 1). The Day/night card counts crew (8 / 1). Both are labelled just "Day", so the page appears to contradict itself.

F3 · KPI "Deployed on a mission"Medium

A five-colour bar with no legend

Deployed, leave, idle, holiday and on-call free share one 8px bar, and the colours are only named in hover tooltips. On a phone nobody hovers.

F4 · KPI "Overall utilization"Medium

The trend caption mixes two windows

"▲ 7 pts vs the previous 5 days · 14-day trend": two numbers of days in one line, and neither says "last week", which is what people actually compare against.

F5 · History + Engineer workloadMedium

Two large charts for a glance at the month

Together they take about 40% of the page height. Most days the lines are flat, and the engineer chart repeats what its own table shows. This is lighter now that they sit at the end, but they are still long.

F6 · phone toolbarMedium

Four rows of chrome before any number

On a phone, Overview shows a "Board…" select and a "More" button that do nothing here, the chip strip, then Export. On a 390px phone, the status bar starts about 310px down the screen.

F7 · By board on a phoneMedium

Half the table is off-screen

At 390px only Board, Utilized and Missions are visible. Headcount, Assigned, Idle and Contract mix need a sideways scroll that nothing hints at.

F8 · toolbar chip stripLow

Headcounts now appear twice

"All employees 40 · Board One 28 · Board Two 12" in the toolbar shows the same numbers the new board pills carry.

F9 · Leave todayLow

Five rows to say "2 people"

Every leave type gets a row, even at 0. On a normal day, three empty tracks push Host coverage and By service area further down.

F10 · Action queue chipsLow

The area tag repeats on every name

"Worker 15 · AREA1" ×20. When one area covers everyone, the suffix is noise. Grouping chips under their board would also answer "where" without reading the right-hand caption.

F11 · section subtitlesLow

The utilization formula is printed in full, twice

"assigned ÷ (headcount − leave − holiday − free on-call)" sits under By board and in the KPI tile's fallback. It belongs in an ⓘ tooltip, read once.

Directions

Four directions, same data

A builds on what shipped today. B and C rethink what the page is for. D is a structural change that combines with A, B or C.

A

Refined dashboard

Keep today's layout and fix what reads wrong: one attention card, labelled KPIs, consistent day/night units, and History shrunk to a strip of sparklines that opens into the full chart.

What changes

  • Status bar and Action queue merge into one attention card, with names grouped by board and the first five shown.
  • Every KPI says its unit, and Deployed gets a visible legend.
  • Day/night always reads "crew · missions".
  • History becomes three sparklines. The full chart opens on demand.
  • Leave lists only the types in use.

Good for

People who already know the tab. Nothing moves far, so nobody has to learn the page again.

Trade-off

It stays a dashboard you read top to bottom. It doesn't tell you what to do next beyond the idle list.

What changes

  • Board cards become the centre of the page, each with the numbers a board lead checks every morning.
  • Each card links to its board, and the idle names sit on the card itself.
  • On a phone the cards stack one under another, so nothing scrolls sideways (F7).

Good for

Managers who look after several sites and want to compare boards at a glance, especially as boards grow past two or three.

Trade-off

The all-boards picture gets thinner, and the per-card numbers repeat the board's own stats bar. With a single board it looks like an oversized card.

Action inbox

Exception-first. A ranked to-do list takes the wide column, and the numbers move to a narrow side rail. When nothing needs doing, the list shrinks to one green line and the page goes quiet.

What changes

  • The page opens with what to do, ranked by urgency: red means act now, amber means act today, grey means no action needed.
  • Each item has the one button that resolves it.
  • Tomorrow's planning gaps and forecast holds move up from the board onto the Overview.

Good for

Planners who open the Overview first thing to find out what to fix, rather than to report.

Trade-off

It is the biggest change for current users and makes a weaker JPG for LINE, since a to-do list is not a status report. It needs new queries for the planning-gap and holds items.

D

Today | Trends

Split the tab in two. Today fits one desktop screen with no scrolling. Trends gives History and Engineer workload the full width, with their range control at the top. This combines with A, B or C.

What changes

  • A Today / Trends switch sits next to the board selector.
  • Trends loads its range query only when it is opened, so Today gets faster.
  • On a phone, By board becomes one card per board (F7) and the board-only toolbar controls are hidden on Overview (F6).

Good for

Everyone on a phone, and anyone who exports Today as a JPG for LINE, because the image becomes one tidy screen.

Trade-off

History is one click further away, and a manager who wants the day and the month side by side has to switch views.

Whichever direction you pick

Quick wins worth doing anyway

  1. One unit for day/nightStatus bar and Day/night card both say "8 crew · 3 missions" (F2).
  2. Legend under the Deployed barName the colours on the tile, not only in the tooltip (F3).
  3. "vs last week"One window in the utilization caption (F4).
  4. Drop the Overview chip stripThe board pills already carry each headcount (F8).
  5. Hide board-only controls on OverviewThe phone toolbar's "Board…" select and "More" (F6).
  6. Leave: only the types in use"Annual 1 · Sick 1", with zeros folded away (F9).
  7. Group idle names by boardAnd drop the area suffix when one area covers everyone (F10).
  8. Formula in an ⓘ tooltipRead once, not printed twice (F11).

Recommendation

A, with D's Today | Trends split. A fixes the parts that read wrong without moving anything far, and D makes the phone view and the LINE export one screen long at little cost. B is the right next step once there are more than three boards. C is the most useful for planners, but it is a different product. It's worth trying as an extra "My day" view before it replaces anything.

Reply with a letter, or a mix such as "A + D, without the sparkline strip", and I'll build it on this branch.
