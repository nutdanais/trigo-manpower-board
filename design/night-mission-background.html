Night mission card: stripe and fade options

Round 2, building on C (stripes) and F (fade). Only the area behind the employee cards changes. The header band, the engineer colour on the top edge and the NIGHT chip stay as they are. Each option shows a day card next to a night card so you can judge how fast night stands out. Use the buttons to see it the way the export does: small like a JPG on a phone, or grey like a black-and-white printout of the PDF.

Every option was tested in the app's own export engine (html2canvas 1.4.1). It drops CSS repeating stripes completely, so the stripes here are built as small repeating tiles instead. That version comes out the same in the JPG as on screen.
