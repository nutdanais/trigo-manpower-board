Bringing the admins' Mission List into the Missions module

This page reviews the 2026 sheet of 2026_BANGN_Mission_List (338 rows, 41 columns, plus the lookup sheets and the Rule sheet). It recommends making the Missions module the one place the list lives, proposes how the app keeps the monthly suffix and the yearly new number, and gives a column-by-column guide for your meeting with the admin team. Each column shows how it would be entered and who fills it in, and a flag marks every column that needs a special way of entering or editing it. You can mark Keep, Drop or Discuss on each row during the meeting and copy the result back to me.

Phase 1: start the Missions module with 10 columns

The team chose to start with only the columns the app needs now. Phase 1 holds what the app has to know to run the register, Close, the monthly suffix and the year-end number. The other 31 columns stay in the admins' workbook for now. They can come in later without rework, because the mission record they belong to exists from Phase 1. The column guide below marks every row P1, P2 or P3, and the team's remarks are applied.

P1 Now · 10 columns

  • CMission number + suffix · typed once from Etraq, suffix made by the app
  • AMonth · from the suffix
  • BMission No. (Last year) · from the year-end step
  • FCustomer name · in the app today
  • QHost / Location · Host list, in the app today
  • ADTrigo responsible · Engineer, in the app today
  • RStart date · first day on the board
  • SFinish date · set by Close
  • TStatus · Open / Closed, automatic
  • UContinue / Close at month end · in the monthly routine

Plus what the board already keeps (shift, hours, PPE, remark, highlight) and one new tick per suffix: Created in Etraq.

P2 Next · job details · 17

What the engineer knows at mission start: customer code, prescriber, part number and name, problem, defect, system and sub-system, mission type, type of operation, breakpoint, agreed and wished pace, customer and host contacts, work instruction status and Rev.

Mostly the engineer's input. Engineers or admins enter contacts and the work instruction (team remark). Comes once Phase 1 is in daily use.

P3 Later · admin and invoicing · 14

Documents and money: MO/PO/QT status and type, document sent to finance, PO status and PO number, rates, invoicing type, rate/price, management fee, transport cost, Etraq rate profile, risk analysis, remark, and DATA ENTRY (meaning still to confirm).

Admin only and restricted. Comes when the workbook is retired. Until then it stays in Excel.

What changes for the admins in Phase 1

  • The mission number, suffix, status and dates come from the app. Nobody needs to work out the next suffix or update "Running" by hand.
  • The workbook still holds the Phase 2 and 3 columns, so each month admins still add rows to it. The app's Monthly suffix list tells them exactly which rows to add and which to close, and Export gives those rows ready to paste.
  • That copying ends in Phase 2, when the job details move into the app.

Question 1 · Can we integrate it?

Yes. The Missions tab becomes the Mission List, and Excel becomes an export

The Mission Register in the spec already holds a mission's number, host, customer, engineer, status, opening and closing date. The Mission List is that same record with more detail (part, defect, documents, invoicing) and a history of suffixes. It belongs in the same place.

A · Keep Excel, import it

The admins keep the workbook. The app reads it with Bulk edit.

  • Least change for the admins
  • The monthly copy and paste stays
  • Two places to keep in step, and the stale "Running" rows remain

B · The app replaces it Recommended

The Missions tab holds every column worth keeping. Export produces the same sheet layout for the Etraq double-check.

  • One record per mission, with its suffixes underneath
  • The monthly suffix is prepared by the app, so nobody copies rows
  • Real dropdowns, so no typos
  • Commercial columns visible only to admins

C · Split

The app holds the operational columns and the suffixes. Excel keeps the commercial columns.

  • Smaller first build
  • Two files still need the same mission number
  • Fine as a stepping stone to B

How B would be rolled out

  1. Import once. Upload this workbook. The app groups the 338 rows into 160 missions, makes each row a suffix period and keeps the "last year" numbers as links. A preview lists what doesn't match the app before anything is saved: unknown hosts, engineer names, a code with two names.
  2. Run side by side for one month. The admins keep their file and check the app's monthly suffix list and Export against it.
  3. Switch. The workbook becomes read-only history. The export is always available for the Etraq check.
Question 3 · The monthly suffix

Keep one mission, and record its suffixes and yearly numbers under it

Yes, there's a better way than copying rows. The app keeps a single record for the job and treats each suffix as a period of that record, with its own start and end date and PO number. The yearly new number is one more link in the same chain. The Etraq still gets its suffix. People stop copying rows to create it.

Stored once on the mission

Customer, prescriber, part, problem, defect, system, type, host, engineer, contacts, rates, invoicing type, documents. Changing one of these changes it for every later suffix and asks "from which suffix?" (D2 in the spec).

Stored on each suffix

Suffix, month, period start and end, PO number, and possibly breakpoint, MO/PO/QT status and "Created in Etraq". Whether the last three belong here is a meeting question. A suffix is never retyped. It's the next code after the last one (AA → AB … AZ → BA).

The monthly routine in the app

  1. From the last working days of the month, the Missions tab shows Monthly suffix due.
  2. The screen lists every open mission with its next suffix already worked out, and carries the PO number over unless someone types a new one.
  3. For each mission the admin chooses Continue or Close at month end (today's "Able to close" column), and ticks Created in Etraq once it's keyed in.
  4. Start December suffixes opens the new periods on the 1st. Missions not yet ticked stay on a "Not in Etraq yet" list, so nothing is forgotten.
  5. New suffix now on a single mission handles the mid-month case (a new PO, a new breakpoint), with the reason noted.

How it fits the year end

  • The December → January routine also asks for the new yearly number, typed from the Etraq. It's the same screen and the same check as the year-end renumber in the spec, so the admins learn one routine.
  • A mission continuing into 2027 starts again at .AA under its new number, and its 2026 number fills "Mission No. (Last year)" automatically.
  • Days, crew and the Host Record keep the number and suffix that were in force on each day, so history reads as it did.
  • Searching either the old or the new number finds the whole chain.
Question 2 · How each column is entered

Six ways of entering a value

Every column in the guide below is given one of these. The aim is that the admins type only what nobody else knows, and the app either fills in or offers a list for the rest.

AAutomatic

Nobody types it. The app fills it from the suffix routine, Close or the year end.

Month, Last-year number, Suffix, Status, Finish date

BPick a record

Chosen from a list the app already keeps (Host list, users). One spelling, everywhere.

Host, Trigo responsible

CDropdown

A fixed list kept in Settings by an admin, like today's lookup sheets. It can have a default.

Mission type, Operation, Defect, System → Sub-system, MO/PO/QT, WI, Invoicing, fees, ETRAQ profile, Risk, PO status

DValue with rules

A number, amount or code the app checks: unit, currency, format, or "TBC" / "Waiting".

Mission number, Customer code, Breakpoint, Pace, Rate, Rate/price lines, PO number

EFree text

Written in a box, searchable, with no checks.

Customer name, Prescriber, Part number, Part name, Problem, Transport cost, Remark (as notes)

FContacts

Names and emails kept on the customer or host they belong to, entered once and reused by every mission there.

Customer contact, Host contact
Meeting guide · 41 columns

Column-by-column checklist

Use this table as the meeting checklist: go row by row, tick ✓ when a column has been discussed and set the Team decision. Suggested is my recommendation. None of the suggestions removes a column from view or from the Excel export. They only change who enters the value:

  • Already in app The app has this field today. It's kept and shown as now.
  • Keep · add A new field in the app, typed or picked by the owner shown.
  • Automatic · still shown Nobody types it any more. The app fills it in, but it's still shown on the mission, searchable and in the export.
  • Pre-filled default The field stays, already filled with the value used almost every time. Change it only when it's different. Still shown and exported.
  • Ask in meeting Its meaning isn't clear from the file.

Drop in the Team decision column is the only choice that really removes a column: it's left out of the app and the export. Status says how the column relates to the app today. Entry is one of the six ways above, and Owner is who fills it in. Flag marks columns that need a Special way of entering or editing them, Attention for a data problem the file shows, or Ask when only the admins know the answer. Effect on the plan

What changes in the Mission Register spec

Number

A mission number becomes PREFIX.YY.NNNN plus a suffix .AA–.ZZ (Q3). The register's identity is the yearly number. The suffix belongs to the period, and the board and history store the full number in force on each day.

Release 1

Phase 1: the register with its 10 columns, Close, and room for suffix periods in the table from the start, so nothing is migrated twice.

Release 2

The year-end renumber becomes the monthly suffix routine, with the January run also asking for new yearly numbers. It's still needed before 1 December if we want December's suffixes made by the app, or before 1 January at the latest.

Release 3

Phase 2 job details (17 columns, with the lookup lists in Settings), then Phase 3 admin and invoicing (14 columns, admin only), the one-off import of this workbook and the Export in its full layout. There is no Customer list (team decision).
