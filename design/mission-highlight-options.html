New on the board: highlight a mission

Make a mission stand out for everyone looking at the day's board: a new customer, a focus activity, a mission short of people, or any reason you type. Then show only the highlighted missions with one switch.

Version 2026-10-28cLive on every board. Set it from a computer or a phone.

What it looks like

A highlighted card gets a gold frame and a gold tag at the top that says why. The top edge keeps the engineer's colour, so you still see whose mission it is.

Try it: right-click any card on the sample board (on a phone, tap its header) to change or remove its highlight.

How to use it

Right-click the mission card

Choose Highlight in the card's menu. On a phone, hold the card's header. You need edit rights on the board.

Pick the reason

New customer, Focus activity and Not enough manpower are one tap. The current reason has a tick, and Remove highlight takes it off.

Or say it in your own words

Other… asks for a short note. It shows on the card exactly as typed.

Show only the highlighted missions

Open Filter & sort and turn on Highlighted only. Every other mission fades back and the highlighted ones move to the top. It counts on the Filter & sort button, and Clear all turns it off.

Good to know

A highlight is for that dayCarry over to the next day, Duplicate mission, Copy to board and Copy from weekday all start without it. Editing or moving the mission keeps it.
Who can set itAnyone who can edit the board. People who can only view the board still see the highlights. Forecast days don't offer it.
In pictures and printThe gold frame and tag show in the board's JPG and PDF exports, so the crew see them in LINE too.
Thai and EnglishThe reasons follow the app's language (ลูกค้าใหม่, งานที่ต้องเน้น, กำลังคนไม่พอ). A note typed for Other stays as typed.
How we chose this look Five options, E picked

Engineer colours already use every hue, and night cards already use the body, so a highlight could not be a colour alone. These were the options. E (frame + tag) was picked because it stands out from across the room and says why in words, which still reads in the JPG.
