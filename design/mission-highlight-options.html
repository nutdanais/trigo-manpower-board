Highlighting a mission card

Engineers want to mark some missions so they stand out on the board: a new customer, a focus activity, or another reason. Below: what the card already uses, how to turn a highlight on with a right-click, five looks to choose from, and a recommendation. Every board below is live. Right-click any card to highlight it or remove the highlight.

What the card already uses

A highlight has to use a signal the card is not already using, or it will be confused with something else.

Top edge and header washThe engineer's colour. Engineer colours cover every hue (gold, green, pink, navy and more), so a highlight cannot be colour alone.
BodyNight missions get the midnight-blue mat. A tinted body for highlight would fight it.
Whole-card fadeFilters fade non-matching cards to 18%. A highlighted card should still fade when it does not match.
Amber outlineUsed for 1.6 s when the board jumps to a card. Short-lived, but a permanent amber frame would look like it got stuck.
Free to useThe card's frame (sides and bottom), the left edge, and a short text label. A label is the only thing that says why.
Picture of the boardThe JPG export can't draw inset shadows, outlines or repeating gradients. Every option here uses borders, outer shadows and flat fills only, so it shows the same in the JPG and PDF.

Turning it on: right-click the card

The card's right-click menu already exists (Edit, Duplicate, Move, Crew, Copy, Hide, Delete). Highlight goes in as one more item with its own sub-menu, so it takes no space on the board. On a phone, the same menu opens by holding the header, as today.

Three reasons, one look. New customer and Focus activity are one tap. Other asks for a short note (up to 40 characters) that shows on the card.

Every highlight looks the same; only the words change. Giving each reason its own colour would compete with the engineer colours, and nobody would remember which colour means what.

Remove highlight appears once a card is highlighted, and a tick marks the current reason.

Highlight colour Right-click a card to change its highlight. Tap the header on a phone.

Recommendation: E, Frame + tab, in gold

The frame makes a highlighted card stand out from across the room without touching the engineer's colour. The tab under the engineer edge says why in plain words, which is the part engineers asked for ("new customer", "focus activity"), and it still reads in the exported JPG, where there is no tooltip. Gold reads as a highlighter pen. The app already uses amber for a short warning flash, but gold is a different shade, comes with a star and a label, and stays put. If gold feels too close to that warning, Rose is the next best choice: no screen in the app uses it.

Look at M-117 in every option: its engineer, Wanida, is gold too, so in gold the frame and the engineer edge blend into one colour. Engineers can pick any colour, so whichever accent we choose, some engineer will share it. This is why the tab with words matters more than the colour, and why A and D, which have no words, are weaker. Switch to Rose to see the same card with no clash.

If engineers expect to highlight most of the board on some days, C (Badge) is the quiet fallback. When every card is highlighted, none of them stands out.

Worth adding with it (optional)

Filter: Highlighted onlyOne more choice in the board's existing filter. It reuses the 18% fade, so the highlighted missions are left standing out on their own. No new UI.
Copy as text carries itThe LINE text from "Copy as text" starts with "★ New customer" (or the note), so the crew get the same message in the chat.
Edit mission shows it tooA Highlight field in the mission editor, for anyone who doesn't right-click. Same three reasons and the note.
Who can set itAnyone who can edit the board, the same rule as Hide. A board viewer sees the highlight but gets no menu item.

Before we build: three questions

  1. Today only, or until removed? Missions are saved per day. Should a highlight copy over to the next day when the plan is copied, or start fresh each day?
  2. Duplicate mission: should the copy keep the highlight? I suggest no.
  3. Order on the board: leave highlighted cards where they are, or move them to the top of the board (like matching cards move up when a filter is on)?
