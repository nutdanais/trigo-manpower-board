Mark the head of a crew

Decided 16 Nov 2026: option A. Corner badge, in teal, exactly one head per mission. Built in the app, where it is called Leader (right-click a person on a mission → Make Leader), and shown on the Org Chart too. The other options are kept below for the record.

Five ways to show which person leads a mission's crew on the day's board. The head is set from the employee card's right-click menu (on a phone, hold the card). Every board on this page works: right-click any card in a crew to make that person the head, or to remove it.

Accent Show Right-click a crew card to change the head

What the mark has to work around

The card is full116 × 70 px with three rows: first name and TRIGO ID, surname, role and area. Three cards must still fit a mission column, so the mark can't make the card bigger.
Most colours already mean something Navy: selected Gold: highlighted mission Green flash: search hit Tan dashed: on-call
Area chips use blue, purple, orange and green, so the default accent here is teal.
"Team Leader" already existsTL is a position, fixed in the profile. Crew head is a role for the day on one mission: a Senior Inspector can lead today and a TL can be a crew member tomorrow. The mark must not look like the TL chip.
It must survive the JPG exportCrews see the board as a picture in LINE, so the mark can't rely on hover or a tooltip, and it must read on the night missions' dark blue body.

In the right-click menu

One new item, just under Profile, only for someone placed on a mission. It isn't shown for Standby, leave, on-call pools, forecasts or a multi-selection, and anyone who can only view the board doesn't get it.

How it would behave (any option)

One head per crew, for that dayMaking someone head replaces the current one. Moving the head off the mission, or to leave or Standby, clears it. Carry over to the next day keeps it, like the crew itself.
Head first in the crewThe head's card moves to the front of the mission's people. All boards on this page do this. It also keeps option E's tab clear of other cards.
Head's name in the mission headerShown in option E as an add-on: "♛ ณัฐพล" after the engineer, so the header alone says who to call. It can go with any option.
Where it is storedOne field on the mission (the head's employee), so a crew can never have two heads. The demo at /?demo would get the same field in its sample data, so the preview shows it.
ThaiMenu: ตั้งเป็นหัวหน้าทีม / ยกเลิกหัวหน้าทีม. Tab and chip: หัวหน้า (fits the same width as HEAD at this size).
ExportsShown in the board's JPG and PDF exports. The Excel export could add a "Head" column.

To decide

  1. Which option, A to E? My pick is E. Frame + tab: it says "HEAD" in words, keeps every chip the card has today, and doesn't clash with selection, on-call or the gold mission highlight.
  2. Which accent: teal, TRIGO green or charcoal? Green is the brand colour, but the search flash and the Sorting area are green too.
  3. Exactly one head per crew, or allow more than one on large crews?
  4. Add the head's name to the mission header too?
