Hide/Unhide missions, redesigned

The current dialog is a 440px list of checkboxes. On a busy board, the missions you most want to hide, the ones with nobody assigned, look the same as every other row. This page proposes three dialog layouts that put those missions first, plus three ways to hide an empty mission straight from its card. Every mockup is live: tick, search, filter and save. The desktop and phone frames in each direction share one state.

What you asked for, and where it is

  1. Wider dialog on desktop, full-screen on phone (directions A, B, C)
  2. Suggest missions with 0 assigned when the dialog opens (in every direction)
  3. Where a "Hide" chip goes on an empty card, and how it looks (H1, H2, H3)
  4. Pick your directions and copy the decision back to me

Sample day used everywhere: Rayong board, Mon 12 Oct 2026. 14 missions: 12 on the board (4 of them with no crew) and 2 already hidden. Names and numbers are placeholders.

Asks 1 and 2

Three dialog directions

All three share the same frame: min(920px, 94vw) wide and 86vh tall on desktop, with a fixed header and footer and only the list scrolling. At 640px and below the dialog goes full-screen (100vw × 100dvh, no rounded corners) with 40px tap targets and a footer padded for the phone's home bar. In every direction, missions with no crew are suggested first and can be selected in one tap. The footer always says what Save will do, including how many people go back to Standby.

A

Grouped list

Recommended

One table split into three groups: Suggested · no crew at the top (amber), then On the board, then Hidden. Groups follow the saved state, so a row stays where it is when you tick it and shows "Will hide" in place. On a phone each row becomes a two-line item with a large checkbox on the right.

Desktop 1000 × 640
Phone 375 × 740, full-screen

Why it works

  • Closest to today. Same tick-to-hide model, so nobody needs retraining.
  • "Select all 4" clears the whole suggestion in one tap.
  • Rows never move while you tick, so you keep your place.

Watch-outs

  • The densest of the three. It needs the column layout to stay readable on desktop.
  • The checkbox meaning (ticked = hidden) still has to be learned. The tags help.

Build notes

  • Reuses openHideMissionsModal(). The groups are three filters over getPlan().missions.
  • Crew count uses this board's roster, like boardStats().unstaffed.
  • One container query switches desktop rows to phone rows.
B

On the board | Hidden

Two panes. The left pane shows what is on the board, with the no-crew missions grouped first and a "Hide all 4" button. The right pane shows what is hidden. Each row has one verb, Hide or Unhide, and moves to the other pane right away (outlined until you save). On a phone the panes become two tabs.

Desktop 1000 × 640
Phone 375 × 740, full-screen

Why it works

  • No checkbox to decode. The button says what happens.
  • You see the resulting board and the hidden list side by side.
  • Unhiding is easy to find because hidden missions have their own pane.

Watch-outs

  • Rows jump between panes, which can be disorienting in a long list.
  • On a phone the hidden tab is one tap away, so unhiding is less visible.

Build notes

  • Same save path (setMissionsHidden twice). Only the markup changes.
  • Phone tabs: one data-tab attribute, CSS only.
C

Mini board

The dialog mirrors the board itself: small mission cards with the engineer's colour edge, in board order. Empty cards have an amber dashed outline and a "No crew" label in their body. Tap a card to hide it (it greys out). A banner offers "Select 4 with no crew". On a phone the grid is two columns.

Desktop 1000 × 640
Phone 375 × 740, full-screen

Why it works

  • Matches the board people already know (the "magnet board" principle).
  • An empty card looks empty, so the suggestion explains itself.

Watch-outs

  • Fits the fewest missions per screen. Slow on boards with 30+ missions.
  • Tap-to-toggle on a whole card is easy to trigger by accident.
  • Shows the least detail (no PPE or engineer phone).

Build notes

  • New mini-card renderer, styled from .mission-card tokens.
  • Needs its own keyboard handling (cards are buttons with aria-pressed).
Ask 3

A "Hide" action on a mission card with no crew

These are replicas of the real mission card, a day and a night card for each option, both with nobody assigned. Click Hide to see what happens: the card leaves the board right away, with an Undo. No confirmation is needed because nobody goes back to Standby, which matches how hideMission() already behaves for empty missions.

Rules for any option

When it showsThe mission has 0 crew from this board, and the viewer can edit this board on this date. Never on a forecast, a read-only past date, or a locked day.
What it doesHides the mission at once and shows a toast: "M-2410-035 hidden · Undo". The toolbar label updates to "Hidden 3" and the badge count goes down.
Drag and drop still worksThe card body stays a drop zone. Dropping someone on the card gives it crew, and the Hide action disappears.
Kept out of exportsMarked data-html2canvas-ignore and hidden in print, so the JPG and PDF of the board never show it.
Does not open the editorThe header opens the mission editor, so the action stops the click from reaching it. H1 avoids the problem because it sits in the body.
Accessible name"Hide mission M-2410-035 (no crew)". At least 28px tall with the mouse and 36px in touch mode.
