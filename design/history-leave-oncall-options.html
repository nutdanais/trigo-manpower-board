Leave and on-call in History

Three ways to show leave, as a total and by type, and how many on-call people were deployed each day. Every mockup is live: pick a tile, switch a leave type on or off, and hover a day for its numbers.

What History has today

  • Four tiles: Utilization, Deployed / day, Standby / day and Missions / day. Pick one to chart it day by day with its average. They follow the range presets and the board selector, and leave out weekends, holidays and boards nobody planned.
  • The figures behind it already count on-call people on a mission each day (oncallAssigned) and the on-call roster (oncallHeadcount), but nothing shows them.
  • Leave comes in one row per person with its type (Annual, Sick, Business, Unpaid, Exchange Working Day). It is added into one total, so the split by type is lost at the very end. Keeping the type is a few lines in cloud.js, with no database change.

My recommendation

A, with B's by-type table

Start with Option A: two more tiles in the History you already have. It is the smallest change and works the same way people already use History. Under the leave chart, add B's small by-type table (days, average per day, share, people). It answers "by type" with numbers you can read off, and the legend chips can't do that alone. Keep C (calendar) for later, if planners ask which days were heavy.

  • You can't see leave and on-call side by side. You flip between them.

History keeps its four tiles. Two new sections follow Engineer workload and use the same range and board: Leave and On-call deployment. Each has headline figures, a chart that is always visible, and a table. That includes who was called in most, which matters for keeping on-call work fair.

C

A calendar of the range

One cell per day, coloured by how many people were on leave, with the on-call count in orange underneath. The chips filter the leave count by type. It reads like the planning calendar and shows at a glance which days were heavy, such as the days next to a holiday.

Questions before I build it

  1. Exchange Working Day. Decided It counts in the leave total, as its own type next to Annual, Sick, Business and Unpaid, as the mockups show. Its chip can still hide it.
  2. "On-call inspectors". Decided Everyone on an on-call contract, the dashed OC cards, whatever their position. The mockups already count it this way.
  3. Leave on a day the board wasn't planned. Decided Only planned boards count. A board-day with no missions is left out of leave and on-call too, like every other History figure, and is named in the "Not planned" line under the tiles.
  4. Names. Decided Show the per-person lists (most days on leave, most called in). They use the same Roles permission as History, so whoever can see History sees the names. With option A they go under the Leave and On-call charts.
  5. Unit. The figures are person-days (3 people on leave for 2 days = 6). The table also shows how many different people took each type. Is that the right pair?
