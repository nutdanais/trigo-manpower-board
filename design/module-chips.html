Status chips, one tab at a time

Click a tab in the navy bar to switch tabs. The badge on a tab is its worst chip, so a problem shows from any tab. Click a chip to see what it does: it narrows the page to the people, missions or days it counts. Click it again to clear. Numbers are made up.

2 on Standby

Red: fix it. Something is wrong with today's plan or the next working days. It puts a red badge on the tab.

7 missing TRIGO ID

Amber: check it. Not wrong yet, but incomplete or tight. It puts an amber badge on the tab if there is no red.

Plan complete

Green: all clear. Shown only when there is no red or amber chip, so a quiet tab still confirms it was checked.

3 missions hidden

Grey: for information. Never a badge. Still clickable when it filters something.

What each chip counts and does

A chip with 0 is not shown. At most four chips per tab, worst first.

TabChipCountsClick
  • One place. Chips sit at the start of the summary line under the toolbar, before the plain numbers.
  • Same shape everywhere. 28px high, 6px square-ish corners, a small square colour key, then the number in bold. No pills.
  • Colour is never the only signal. The words always say what is wrong ("on Standby", "short day").
  • Live. Chips and badges update with Realtime, like the stats do now.
  • Not in exports. Like the banners, chips are for the planner and are left out of JPG, PDF and Excel.
