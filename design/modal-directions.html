Modal redesign directions

The app has 26 dialogs. 17 of them inherit the default 440 px card, and the nine that do have a width run from 640 to 1080 px. The narrow ones squeeze long forms into a scrolling column. The wide ones are fixed pixel widths that don't use a big screen and don't adapt on a phone. Below: what is wrong today, then four directions, each shown on the same two dialogs (Edit Employee, a long form, and Review & merge forecast, a wide data review) plus a phone view. The sample data is made up.

Shared root causes: (1) one size for everything, so every dialog gets 440 px unless someone hand-writes a width; (2) forms are always one column; (3) there is no phone behaviour except Hide missions, so most dialogs just shrink; (4) the dialog title, form and footer are good, with a sticky header, hairlines and a tinted action bar. That anatomy stays in every direction. The redesign is about size, layout and phone behaviour, not about replacing the markup.
