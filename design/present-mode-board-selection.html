Present mode and board selection

Two proposals for review. Board selection lets the Overview show more than one board at a time. Present mode turns the Overview into something you can put on a projector or a wall screen. Each has several options as working mockups on the same sample day, so you can compare them by using them.

Sample: Wed 04-Nov-2026, four boards, 106 people. Numbers are placeholders, and the mockups are not loaded by the app.

How to review

  1. Board selection: tick boards in A to D and watch the numbers change.
  2. Present mode: step through P1 to P4 with the arrows.
  3. Recommendation, then your decision to copy back to me.

What I assumed

  • You have 4 boards, and "2 out of 4" means you could pick any two of them (I also show a hard cap of 2, option A).
  • Multi-select is for the Overview only. Board, Capacity and the lists keep one board at a time.
  • Present mode is a read-only, large-type view for a meeting room or a wall screen. Tell me if you meant something else.
What the app does today

Multi-select is mostly a picker change

Read from app.js, cloud.js and the Overview handover notes.

1 · Sections already take a listEvery Overview section reads boards = ovScopeBoards(), which already returns an array. The numbers, the attention card and the By board rows sum or list whatever boards are in it.
2 · The selector is one or allThe picker stores a single id (or empty for all) in manpower.ovBoard, per device. The label, the "all boards" wording and the History control Side by side assume one or all.
3 · Rows set the selectorA row in By board or Boards & areas, and a chart in Side by side, sets the selector to that one board. The Report dialog uses the selector as its scope.
4 · Loans cross the lineA loan from a ticked board to an unticked one is only half inside the selection. It needs a label such as "lent outside selection".
5 · No present mode yetThe nearest thing is Export (JPG, PDF), which is static. The Overview is already read-only. The Board tab is editable, so presenting it needs an edit lock.
6 · Names are permissionedov.status and ov.actionQueue decide who sees the headline and the idle names, and ov.history guards per-person figures. A shared screen should not widen this, and needs a Hide names switch.
7 · Live data existsThe cloud layer already subscribes to realtime changes, so a wall display can stay current without polling or a reload.
Proposal 1

Board selection on the Overview

Five ways to pick boards, driving the same sample Overview. Tick, press Only, and compare the summary line. Option C compares two boards instead of adding them up. Option E gives the Period group (History and the sections after it) its own board control, linked to the page by default.

A

Tick list in the existing picker

The Board button keeps its place in toolbar row 1. Its menu gets checkboxes, an All boards row and an Only shortcut per row. The button names the boards you picked.

  • Smallest change to what people know
  • Any mix of 1 to 4 boards, no extra rules
  • Phone: the same list as a bottom sheet
  • The choice hides behind a click (the label must carry it)
Build: small. The saved value becomes a list of ids; one old id still reads correctly.
With a cap, "All boards" stays as its own row and ticking a third board is refused.
B

Board chips under the header

A row of toggle chips sits at the top of the Overview, one per board, plus All. What is on screen is always visible, and one tap changes it.

  • The scope is readable at a glance, and a screenshot shows it
  • One tap per board, no menu
  • Uses a row of width; with 8 or more boards it needs to scroll
  • Replaces the Board button on the Overview only, so that tab differs from the others
Build: small to medium. New row, plus the same saved list as A.
C

Compare two boards

A switch next to the picker: Combined or Compare. Compare takes exactly two boards and shows them as two columns with a difference column, instead of one summed figure. This is the "2 out of 4" idea taken literally.

  • Answers "how is One doing against Three?", which a sum cannot
  • Fits History's Side by side
  • A new layout for every section, not just a filter
  • People, Workforce and Host coverage have no natural two-column form
Build: medium to large. Best added after A, for the sections where a difference means something (summary, By board, History).
D

Saved board groups

People name the groups they look at often, such as "Day crews" or "South". The picker lists All boards, each board and the saved groups. You build a group once by ticking boards, then reuse it with one pick.

  • Fast for recurring combinations, and meeting-ready ("Open Monday review")
  • A group can be shared, so the whole team sees the same scope
  • More to manage: names, edit, delete, boards that leave a group
  • Shared groups need a database table and a permission
Build: medium (device only) to large (shared). Needs A's tick list to build a group.
E

Two scopes: the page, and the Period

The day sections (Overview, In detail) use a simple page scope, as in A. The Period group gets its own board control in its heading: Combined, Side by side or A vs B. It starts linked to the page, so most people never see a second choice. Unlink it to pick different boards.

  • Simple where people look quickly, as rich as needed where they analyse
  • Fits the page's existing split: the Period group already has its own range and Compare with
  • A vs B answers the board-against-board question over time, which C answers only for one day
  • Two scopes on one page need clear labels, or the Report scope is ambiguous
  • One comparison at a time: A vs B turns Compare with (previous period, last year) off
Build: medium to large, in steps: page scope (A), then the linked Period scope with Combined, then A vs B for History and Boards & areas. People, Engineer workload and Workforce only combine.

The page scope is shown as chips here to save room. In the app it is A's tick list. Try: unlink the Period, tick boards, switch to A vs B, then back to Combined and change Compare with.

Proposal 2

Present mode

All four are read-only, use the board selection you already made, and leave with Esc. They differ in who drives, how much you see at once, and how much has to be built. Use the arrows or dots in P2 to P4, and the Hide names switch in P1 and P3.

P1

Clean screen

The same Overview with the banner and toolbar removed, the page at about 125% and full width. Move the pointer to the top edge to get a slim bar with the scope, the date, Hide names and Exit. You scroll as you talk.

  • The least to build, and it is the page people already know
  • Every section is available, including People and History
  • Small details stay small on a projector
  • You scroll live, so the audience watches the page move
Build: small. A body class plus a scale, a hover bar and the full-screen call.
P2

Slides, you drive

One section per screen in large type: Today, Idle people, Boards, Trend, and optionally Host coverage. Arrow keys, a clicker or a tap move on. A start sheet picks the scope, the slides and whether names show.

  • Reads from the back of a room
  • You control the pace, so it suits a stand-up or a review
  • Each slide is built from the page's own functions, so figures cannot drift
  • Slides are a second layout to keep in step with the page
Build: medium. Four or five slide renderers plus the start sheet.
P3

Wall display

The same slides, rotating on their own every 15, 30 or 60 seconds, for a TV in the planning room. Follows today's date, refreshes itself from the live feed, shows a clock and a last-updated time, and pauses when someone presses a key.

  • Nobody has to run it, and it is always current
  • Costs little once P2 exists: a timer and a refresh
  • Needs a kept-awake screen and a signed-in browser that stays open
  • Names on a wall are the biggest privacy question (default hidden)
Build: small on top of P2. In this mockup a slide lasts 6 seconds so you can see it turn.
P4

Spotlight strip

The live Overview stays as it is. A floating strip at the bottom steps through sections, dimming everything except the one you are talking about. You can still click a name or a board, because nothing is replaced.

  • You can drill into a board or a person mid-meeting
  • Nothing new to keep in step, because it is the page
  • Page-sized type and dense tables on a projector
  • The audience sees the whole page, including what you did not mean to show
Build: small to medium. Section anchors, a dim class and the strip.

Rules for every present option

Where it startsA Present button next to Export on the Overview, and in the ⋯ menu on a phone. Board tab: later, with an edit lock (open question 4).
Who mayAnyone who can open the Overview. Present mode never adds a permission: names follow ov.actionQueue, per-person figures follow ov.history, and a section a role cannot see has no slide.
NamesA Hide names switch in the bar. Hidden shows counts and initials-free placeholders. Default: hidden for P3, remembered for the rest.
ScreenUses full screen where the browser allows it and a clean fallback where it does not (iPhone). Asks to keep the screen awake. Thai and English follow the app language.
DateShows the date on screen. P3 can follow "today" so it rolls over at midnight; P1, P2 and P4 stay on the date you were viewing.
ExitEsc, the bar's Exit, or a long press on a touch screen. A wall display needs a deliberate exit so a stray click does not drop it.

My recommendation

A for selection, P2 with P3's timer for presenting

Board selection: A for the page, then E for the Period group. The Overview already handles any list of boards, so "2 of 4" is just a selection, and a cap blocks "3 of 4" without a reason. A cap also makes "All boards" an exception, which is awkward when the whole point of the Overview is the full picture. Then add E: the Period group gets its own scope, linked to the page until someone unlinks it, with Combined, Side by side and A vs B. A vs B covers the board-against-board question over time, so C is only needed if you also want it for a single day. D saved groups can wait until the same pairs keep coming back.

Present mode: P2, with P3's auto-advance as one switch in the same start sheet. That is one build, with two uses: you drive it in a meeting, or you set it to rotate and leave it on a wall. P1 comes free as the first slide's look, so it needs no separate build. P4 is a good fit only if you mostly present on a laptop and want to click through, so I would keep it as a later extra.

  • Build order: A first (small, and P2 reads the same selection), then P2 with its start sheet, then P3's timer, then E in two steps (linked Period scope with Combined, then A vs B), then D if wanted.
  • E rules: the Period scope starts linked; each group heading names its scope; A vs B and Compare with are never on together; the Report names both scopes.
  • Phone: P2's slides work in portrait with one tile per row. The wall display is for a TV, so it is not offered on a phone.
  • Tests: scope list saved and restored, an old single id still works, a deleted board drops out, Thai labels, no sideways scroll at 400 px, and names hidden means no name in the page text.
