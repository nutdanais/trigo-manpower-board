Your requests and what the mockups do

#You askedIn the mockup
1Option B, collapsing on scrollFrames 1, 3 and 4. The summary card folds closed by default; scrolled, the header is one 48pt line.
2+ Mission and + Employee inside ⋯Frame 2. They are the first two items, drawn as the menu's main actions.
3Summary card, Filter and ⋯ on one lineFrames 1 and 6. The card takes the free width; Filter and ⋯ are 44pt squares.

Small corrections I added

Date

  • The weekday sits small above "14 Oct", and the year drops (the picker still shows it), so the whole date is about 110pt wide
  • Arrows stay full 36pt tap targets

Board name

  • Shrinks from 22pt to 18pt to share the line; names up to about 14 characters fit whole
  • A long name shortens with "…"; the chip and date never get squeezed (Frame 6)
  • The chip also reads FORECAST or HOLIDAY, so the Holiday state stays visible after the toggle moves into ⋯

Summary card

  • Closed, it shows assigned/total plus the leave, standby and on-call counts as coloured dots
  • Standby turns red when someone is unassigned
  • On a forecast day it reads "planned" instead of "assigned"

Filter

  • A count badge appears when any filter is on (Frame 6), so a filtered board is never mistaken for the whole board

Leave tabs

  • One row with short names; swipe for the rest
  • Types with people in them come first, so they are always visible

Avatar → "Me"

  • Saves the top-right corner
  • The save dot moves onto it: green when saved, amber while saving, red on error
  • The menu keeps your name and role at the top

Trade-off to accept

  • + Mission is now 2 taps (⋯ then New mission). An empty board still shows a "+ New mission" button in the middle, so a new day starts in one tap.
