Four design directions for the app introduction deck

Each direction shows the same four slides: cover, an app slide, access with QR codes, and installing as a PWA. Pick one, or mix (for example the layout of D with the colours of A), and the full six-slide deck follows.

The Inventory & Quality QR code opens the live address from the repo. The Manpower Board address is not recorded in the repo, so its QR code points to a placeholder marked URL to confirm.

A · MistB · Navy FadeC · Sage and SlateD · Split Panel
Direction A

Mist

Swiss and quiet

Pale blue-grey ground, thin hairlines instead of boxes, one green rule as the only accent. The most restrained option and the closest to the TRIGO design system. Reads well on a projector and in print.

Ground #F3F6F8Faded blue #DCE6EENavy text #062A44Green rule #97C21D
Direction B

Navy Fade

Brand-forward

Navy fading to a soft slate-blue on the opening and the QR slide, light content slides between. Strongest TRIGO identity, with the logo in white on the cover. Cards float on a pale ground.

Navy #0B3A5CFaded slate #9DB4C6Ground #EEF2F6Pale green #E4EDCB
Direction C

Sage and Slate

Soft tiles

A muted sage ground with rounded tiles in faded blue, green and stone. The friendliest option, and the most visibly faded. Each feature sits in its own tile, so it scans quickly on a call.

Sage ground #E6ECE7Slate tile #C9D6E1Green tile #D3DEC7Ink #1F3B4D
Direction D

Split Panel

App on the left, detail on the right

A faded blue panel carries the app identity and the call to action, white space on the right carries the detail. Gives each app a consistent home on the slide, and puts the QR code beside the features.

Panel top #DCE6EEPanel bottom #C2D1DEDetail #FFFFFFAccent text #6E9312
