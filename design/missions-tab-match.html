Missions tab, in line with Manpower and Host

This is the next step after the toolbar fix. Nothing here is built yet: it shows what would change so you can approve it or ask for something different. The + Mission button is the one thing already in the app.

What still feels different

  • The board name is plain text here, a chip on Host, and plain text again on Manpower.
  • Board and Status are two columns that answer one question: where is this mission.
  • Close… and Add to board repeat as buttons down every row. The other tables keep actions out of sight until you hover a row.
  • The tab has no By area chips, the quickest filter on Manpower and Host.
  • Shift is a purple pill and the Board dropdown is a browser control.

What changes

  • 1One board chip everywhere, the one Host uses. Manpower and Missions switch to it.
  • 2Board and Status become one column: the chip, and the state under it. Add to board sits inline on the state that needs it.
  • 3Row actions move behind one ⋯ button that shows on hover. Close mission is in that menu. An alert that suggests closing stays a button on its own.
  • 4By area chips under the tiles, with a colour key beside each host, as on Host.
  • 5Board becomes a filter inside Filters, so the toolbar matches Manpower's: the primary button, search, the switch, Filters.
  • 6Shift is plain text with a moon for nights. Pills are kept for alerts only. An empty result gets a message and a way to clear the filters.

Where I need your call

  • By area chips pick one area at a time, as on Manpower and Host. Say if you want several at once.
  • Board moves into Filters as a multi-select. It no longer scopes the tiles on its own.
  • Close mission moves behind ⋯. If planners close missions daily, I would keep Close visible on hover instead of in a menu.
