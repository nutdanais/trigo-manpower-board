New this month

Mission Leader

Mark who leads the crew on each mission. The Leader gets a teal crown on their card and moves to the front of the crew.

  1. 1
    Right-click a person on a mission

    On a phone, press and hold their card.

  2. 2
    Choose Make Leader

    If the mission already has one, the menu says who will be replaced. One Leader per mission.

  3. 3
    The crown follows the crew

    Carry over brings the Leader along. Moving the Leader to another mission, leave or Standby clears it.

Org Chart shows a Leader chip Org Chart PowerPoint Excel: new Leader column JPG and PDF export Thai: หัวหน้าทีม

Viewers see the crown but cannot change it. To undo, right-click the Leader and choose Remove as Leader. Present mode does not show the Leader yet.

Planning the board

Less hunting for people, fewer wrong numbers, and a clear sign when the day is ready.

Tick when your part is planned

When several engineers plan one board, each ticks their own name once their missions are done.

  • The line shows 1 of 3 planned and names who is still working on it.
  • When everyone has ticked it reads Ready to share with crew.
  • It is a flag only. Nothing gets locked. Admins and managers can tick for anyone.
  • Each day starts empty, and it works on phones too.
PR #131

Find crew who know the host

The app now remembers who has worked at each host, and uses it to help you staff a mission.

  • Bring back the last crew adds the people from the last time, if they are free.
  • Suggest crew picks people with the most days at that host.
  • Sort people by days at this host reorders the people panel.
  • An amber Crew new to this host line warns when nobody on the crew has been there before.
  • Find replacement appears when you move someone from a mission to leave.
PR #127

Mission numbers match Etraq

Every mission number must follow the Etraq format, so the board, the Host record and reports agree.

  • Wrong numbers show in red on the card, with a Fix number button.
  • Not in Etraq yet? Use a temporary number. It is flagged after 3 days as a reminder.
  • Numbers are always saved in capitals, however they are typed.

The new Missions tab

One list of every mission across your boards. It answers which jobs are running, which have no crew, and which need attention.

Open and Closed

Close a mission when the job ends, reopen it if needed. Counts sit on the switch, and the tiles filter.

Alerts under the status

No crew for 25 days, hidden 9 days, off the board, wrong number. You see what needs a look.

This year first

The list opens on this year's missions. A chip brings back earlier years.

Your columns

Columns, Filters, Bulk edit and Export. Hover a row for Close and the ⋯ menu.

Double-click for the whole story

  • Crew per working day, average crew, and everyone who has worked on it.
  • Highlight, Duplicate, Move or copy, Hide and Close from one place.
  • New-year numbers: last year's number stays beside the new one for two weeks.
  • A new Site Admin role for the people who manage the mission list.

Smaller changes

Worth knowing, even if you won't notice them every day.

Demo mode for training#118

Add ?demo to the address to open the app on sample data. Nothing is saved, so trainees can try anything. Each person gets their own copy.

No TRIGO ID alert for on-call people#133

On-call staff no longer show a missing ID. When someone becomes permanent, the app asks for their TRIGO ID.

Host experience is now a setting#121

Settings > Host experience: how many days make someone experienced (5 to begin with) and when a host is at risk (2 or fewer).

Site code length is a setting#130

Settings > Mission numbers: how many letters the site code has (5 to begin with).

On-call list: least called in first#107

In the Overview's On-call view, the people called in least are at the top.

Fixed: Today slide counts#111

Present's Today slide now shows missions and crew separately, like the Overview card.

New top bar#97

The app icon and "Manpower Board" in the top left. The TRIGO logo moved to the bottom of Settings.

Workforce chart filters#109

Click a years-of-service group to show or hide it. The totals follow.
