Approved, with your edits: no engineer phone, PPE or time unless that is what changed; the contract shows only as an OC badge on on-call people. Notes for building it

  • The same card works on desktop and phone. On a phone the mission details, crews and note fold under "More", so the choice and the key facts fit on one screen.
  • On desktop: 1 / 2 or ← / → to choose, Backspace to go back. On a phone: swipe, or the two footer buttons.
  • Fit checks are hints only (green = good, amber = worth a look). They never block a choice.
  • "Keep all" and "Use all" still decide everything at once, then go straight to the reason and confirm cards.
  • Nothing new is stored. Every detail on the card is already in the app; the card just brings it together.
