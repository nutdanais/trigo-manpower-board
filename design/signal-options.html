2 · Review & merge forecast: the workflow

I went through the flow in the code (openMergeReview → renderMerge → PlanDiff.decide → confirm → apply). This is what it asks of a planner today:

Today, in one dialog
  1. Read a legend of four chip states (Same, Needs a decision, Kept, Not used) before starting. The vocabulary comes before the task.
  2. Scan a three-column table (mission · carry-over · forecast) of every mission, including the ones with nothing to decide. Clashes are mixed in with rows that need nothing.
  3. Click a person chip on the side you want to keep. Hovering pairs the two chips. The click target is small, and nothing says "click to choose".
  4. When you don't use someone's forecast, pick or type a reason, because they get an alert.
  5. "Apply" stays locked until every clash has a side, then a confirm step shows the summary.

The data and rules stay the same in every option below. Missions added in the forecast and people coming off Standby still go in without a decision, and every real clash still needs a side. Only the way you work through them changes. All three are workbenches, the large surface this dialog already is.
