Signal: a calmer, more modern TRIGO board

This is my own proposal, not based on the Settings page. It restyles the whole app, board first, and treats dialogs as part of the planning surface instead of interruptions. Everything in section 3 is clickable. The earlier Settings-based modal proposal stays available as the baseline for comparison (section 8).

1 · What the app looks like today

These are screenshots of the real app running with sample data, so every finding is something you can see.

Board today

New mission today

  1. Four bands of chrome before any content. Top bar, module tabs, board tabs with actions, then stat tiles: about 215 px of a 900 px screen is controls. The mission cards start below the fold on a laptop.
  2. Nine controls of equal weight. New Mission, New Employee, Engineer, Host, Customer, Shift, Hide/Unhide, Sort and Reset all look like buttons. The four filters are not actions, and nothing says which control matters most.
  3. Five leave rows always open. Annual, Sick, Business, Unpaid and Exchange day each take a full-width row labelled "Drag here". On most days four of them are empty and still cost about 170 px.
  4. Grey tiles on a grey canvas. People cards are mid-grey with a blue area pill, on a blue-grey page. The name is the smallest strong thing on the card, and solid versus dashed outlines carry meaning.
  5. Inconsistent lighting. Mission headers are pastel washes of each engineer's colour, and a night mission gets a near-black block under the header. It reads as an error state, not as a shift.
  6. One typeface at one weight range. Labels sit at 10 to 11 px, numbers in the stat tiles and mission cards are not set apart from text, and the logo bar, tabs and tiles use different corner radii.
  7. Dialogs hide the work. New Mission is a 440 px card that scrolls and covers the board you are planning. All 26 dialogs share that shell whether they hold two fields or a 12-row review.

2 · The direction: Signal

A planning board is an instrument you read all day. Signal makes the data loud and the chrome quiet, and keeps the board in view while you edit.

Five principles

1. The board never leaves. Edits happen in a side inspector you can drag cards past. Only big reviews take the whole screen.
2. One primary action per surface. One filled "New" button. Everything else is quiet.
3. Data over decoration. Large tabular numerals, name first, no grey-on-grey.
4. Structure from space and tint, not from a border on every box.
5. One motion language. 160 to 300 ms, a single easing, nothing animates layout.

What changes, what stays

Changes: Geist for Latin and numbers, Geist Mono for mission numbers and counts. A cooler, lighter canvas. Chrome cut from four bands to three slim ones. Leave zones become a dock. People become name-first chips with squircle initials. Dialogs become an inspector, a workbench, or a small dialog.
Stays: TRIGO navy and the green mark, Noto Sans Thai for Thai names (Geist falls back to it automatically), the vocabulary, every id the tests use, drag and drop, the printed and image export.
One accent: navy is the only action colour. Green appears only in the logo bar and as the avatar tint, never as a button.

Shape, shadow, motion

Radii: 6 for chips and tags, 8 for controls and people chips, 12 for cards and panels, 14 for the workbench. Smaller inside, softer outside.
Shadows: tinted navy, never grey. Cards rest on a 1 px ring. Only floating panels get the large shadow.
Motion: 160 ms for hover and press, 300 ms for panels, one easing curve. Only transform and opacity move. Reduced-motion turns all of it off.
Dark mode: same structure, navy lifts to #2b73b8, text to #e8eef5. Flip the toggle at the top.

8 · How I would roll it out

Small steps, each shippable on its own and each re-running the existing tests, so nothing breaks the board.

PhaseWhatTouchesRisk
1 Tokens and typeFont swap, canvas, borders, radii, shadows, tabular numerals. No layout changestyles.css onlyLow Re-check export image text fits
2 ChromeTabs into the top bar, board switcher and date in one toolbar, one New button, filters into one popover, pulse strip, leave dockindex.html, render code in app.js, CSSMedium Keep every id the e2e tests use
3 CardsMission card and people chips, night shift tag, engineer railcard render in app.js, CSSMedium Drag and drop, and the JPG and print export, must look the same
4 InspectorMission, Employee, Host, User and the small record forms move into the side inspectordialog markup and open/close codeMedium Focus handling and the unsaved-changes guard
5 Workbench and paletteReview & merge as a queue, other large dialogs on the same shell, Ctrl K palettemerge render code, CSS, new palette codeMedium The merge model in planning.js is untouched
One design risk to decide now: collapsing five leave rows into a dock means a drop target is smaller until you start dragging. My plan is that the dock expands into the five full-size zones the moment a card is picked up, then collapses again. That needs a quick usability check with a real planner before phase 2.
