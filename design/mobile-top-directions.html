My pick: C

  • A's layout loses nothing at a glance; all four numbers stay visible
  • Collapsing gives the most room on a long board, where it matters most
  • The + and ⋯ stay in the sticky line, so you never scroll back up to add

Choose B if…

  • people rarely read the area counts or Day/Night on a phone
  • you want the calmest screen; it saves no more room than A, and the details cost one extra tap
