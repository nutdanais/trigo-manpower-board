Soft Fade: four more variations

Each variation keeps the Geist type, the navy, sky and green fades and the real screenshots from sample A (the light one you liked), and changes how the colour is used. Every variation shows the same two slides, the cover and an app slide, so they are easy to compare. Pick one, or mix parts, for example C's panel with B's dark cover.
