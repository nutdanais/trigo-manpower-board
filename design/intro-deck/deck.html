TRIGO apps intro deck

Nine slides introducing the Manpower Board and the Inventory & Quality System, in the Control Room style. Screenshots come from both apps running on their sample data. Talking points sit under each slide.

Global Quality Solution

Two apps for the work we do every day.

The Manpower Board plans who goes where. The Inventory & Quality System tracks tools, parts and consumables. Both run in the browser and on your phone.

Manpower Board

Tomorrow's crew, planned on one board.

The magnet board, made digital. Drag people onto missions, leave or standby, and everyone sees the same plan, live.

  • drag_pan

2 · Manpower: the daily boardEach card is a person, each column a mission with host, customer, shift and engineer. The tiles on top always add up: assigned plus leave plus standby equals total. The phone shows a day being merged: the carried-over plan beside the forecast, with each difference waiting for a decision.
