Airy (D): six colour palettes

Every palette keeps the Airy layout: near-white slide, thin headline, outline pills and floating windows. Only the colour changes: the soft glow behind the screenshots, the gradient on the key words, the eyebrow line and the icons. You could also give each app its own palette, for example Lagoon for the Manpower Board and Sunrise for Inventory & Quality, with Aurora on the cover and the closing slide.

D1Sky and leaf

The original D: TRIGO blue and green.

Global Quality Solution

Two apps for the work we do every day.

The Manpower Board plans who goes where. The Inventory & Quality System tracks tools, parts and consumables. Both run in the browser and on your phone.
