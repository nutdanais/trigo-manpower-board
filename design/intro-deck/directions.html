TRIGO apps intro deck: pick a direction

Three ways to style a short deck for the Manpower Board and the Inventory & Quality System. Each direction shows the same three sample slides: the Manpower intro, the Inventory features and benefits, and a shared slide on installing both apps on a phone. The final deck will have 2 to 3 slides per app in the direction you choose.

How they compare

DirectionFeelBest atWatch out for
A Control RoomBold, product launchA projected room with management present; makes the apps look substantialScreenshots get cropped, so engineers see less of how the screen works
B Annotated ScreensClear, instructionalEngineers who will use the apps tomorrow; each slide is also a quick referenceFeels more like training than an introduction to management
C Two AudiencesBalanced, practicalYour mixed room: every app slide says what it does for engineers and for managersBusiest layout of the three, so copy has to stay short

My recommendation: C, because your brief asks for benefits to engineers and to management, and C puts both on every app slide. A could still lend its dark cover as the opening slide.

You can also mix them, for example a C deck with B's annotated screen as the second slide for each app.
