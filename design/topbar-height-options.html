Matching Inventory's top bar to Manpower's

Manpower stays as it is and Inventory changes. Every bar below is drawn at its real CSS pixel size, so a height you see here is the height you get. The dashed red line is Manpower's bottom edge.

My suggestion
Option A: make Inventory's top bar 55 px tall, the same as Manpower's.
  • It fits. Inventory's tallest control is the Warehouse picker. At 34 px, with 10 px above and below, it fills the bar exactly (34 + 10 + 10 = 54, plus a 1 px line).
  • It fixes a mismatch Inventory already has. Today the sidebar's logo row is about 6 px shorter than the header, so the two dividers don't meet. Both would become 55 px.
  • Inventory gets 14 px of height back on every screen, which helps a data-heavy app.
  • Nothing changes in Manpower, and the controls end up the same size in both apps (34 px controls, 32 px avatar).

Option A · Match Manpower exactly

Recommended

Gains

  • Identical bar height and identical control sizes in both apps.
  • Sidebar logo row and header line up at 55 px.
  • 14 px more room for content on every Inventory page.
  • No change to Manpower.

Costs

  • The Warehouse picker shrinks from 44 to 34 px, a smaller click target.
  • On touch screens a 40 px control still fits (40 + 7 + 7 = 54), so the bar can stay 55 px there too.

Option B · Same height, larger controls

Fallback

Gains

  • The bar height still matches, so switching apps doesn't move anything.
  • Controls stay a little easier to hit than Option A.

Costs

  • Controls are 4 px bigger than Manpower's, so the two apps look close but not identical.
  • Only 8 px of space above and below the picker, against Manpower's 10 px.

Option C · Meet in the middle at 62 px

Changes both apps

Gains

  • Neither app takes the whole change: Inventory gives up 7 px and Manpower takes 7 px.
  • Both bars have roomier controls (38 px in Manpower, 40 px in Inventory).

Costs

  • Manpower's sticky header, which stays on screen while a long board scrolls, grows by 7 px on every board page.
  • Two apps to change and test instead of one.
  • You said Manpower is the reference, so this works against that.

If you choose Option A, this is what changes

All in the Inventory app. The Manpower Board's files are not touched. I haven't changed anything yet.

  • Top bar: content height 68 → 54 px (the line adds 1 px).
  • Warehouse picker: 44 → 34 px tall. Avatar: 36 → 32 px. Bell and cart hit areas: 34 px.
  • Sidebar logo row: 63 → 55 px, so its divider meets the header line.
  • The "New address" banner and the page content below keep their sizes and move up 14 px.
  • Anything in Inventory that sticks below the bar (for example a sticky table header offset by the old height) needs its offset updated to 55 px. I'd check this when implementing.
