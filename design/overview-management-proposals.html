Part 2

Your three asks

All three use data the app already loads. Nothing new has to be recorded.

1 · One engineer's workload over the period

Your askData ready

Engineer workload keeps its ranked table, and a row (or the picker) opens this view. Pick a measure tile, then group by day, week or month. The dashed line is the average engineer, so you can see whether this engineer runs above or below the team.

Engineer workload · one engineer

Hosts this engineer ran missions at

Also possible: pick two engineers to compare them on the same chart, and click a column to open that day's board filtered to the engineer.

2 · Worked days : Leave days, per person

Your askData ready

This goes in History's Leave view, after or in place of "Most days on leave". It counts each person's days over the History range, on the same planned working days as everything else. Leave share = leave ÷ (worked + leave + standby), so a person's figure isn't hidden by how many days they were available. Sort by any column.

Worked vs leave, per person

WorkedLeaveStandby 20–30% or over 30% of available days on leave
The starting thresholds are 20% (amber) and 30% (red), for everyone. They would be a setting, so they can be changed later or set apart for on-call staff. On-call people have no Standby, so their split is worked vs leave vs not called.

3 · The same in the Manpower List

Your askData ready

The Manpower List already fetches each person's worked and leave dates for its 30D utilization column, so three columns cost nothing extra: Worked, Leave and Worked : Leave. They would be sortable and filterable, and included in the Excel export. A period switch (30D / 90D / YTD) applies to all four. The new columns are tinted green below.

Part 3

All ideas

Grouped by the question each one answers for management. Data ready means the app already stores it. Small change means a small addition to what is stored, or a new setting. New data means someone would have to start recording something. Effort: S = a day or two, M = about a week, L = more.
