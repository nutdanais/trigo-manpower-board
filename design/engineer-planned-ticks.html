“My part is planned” ticks

A light check-in on the board: every engineer with a mission on today's board gets a tick box by their name. Tick it when your missions are planned. When every name is ticked, the board says it is ready to share with the crew. Nobody is blocked by it. It only lets the others know.

What the board already gives us

The list builds itselfThe names are the engineers on this board's missions for this date. Add a mission for a new engineer and their name appears. Remove their last mission and the name goes.
The app knows which name is yoursEngineer records are linked to login accounts (Settings → Engineers), so your own name can be highlighted and is the one you tick.
One plan per board per dayTicks belong to “Rayong, Thu 12 Nov”. Tomorrow starts with no ticks, and Carry over does not copy them.
Live for everyoneMissions and crew already sync between people in real time. Ticks would use the same sync, so the last engineer sees the others' ticks without reloading.

Rules for every option

  • Tick your own name. Admins and managers can also tick for someone, for example an engineer on site with no signal. The tooltip then says who ticked and when.
  • Untick anytime. It is a flag, not an approval. Nothing is locked, and Export and Present keep working with or without ticks.
  • Ready to share shows when every listed engineer has ticked. Before that it shows “1 of 3 planned”.
  • On screen only. The ticks never appear in the exported JPG, PDF or Excel the crew receives.
  • Past days and locked days show their ticks but cannot be changed, like the rest of the board.

With 9 engineers on one board

A name chip is about 125px wide, so 9 chips need about 1,150px. On a 1400px screen the numbers row is about 1,130px wide, and the stats already fill half of it. A as first drawn: every name as a chip

The chips no longer fit beside the numbers. They get a line of their own and still wrap. On a phone they take 5 rows and push the first mission card well down.

A1 · Initials tiles

Nine small tiles in the engineers' own colours. A tile fills in and gets a green check when that engineer ticks. Your tile is outlined, and your own tick button keeps your full name. Hover over a tile (or long-press on a phone) to see the name. It fits on the “By area” line. The drawback is that people have to recognise each other by initials and colour.

A2 · Who's left, by name New pick

Your own tick, the count, and the full names of the engineers who have not ticked yet: three at most (two on a phone), then “+2 more”. The line gets shorter as people tick, until it says “Ready to share with crew”. “All 9” opens the full list, where you can see or change any tick. It answers the last engineer's real question: who are we still waiting for?

Where

On the right of the row with Total, Assigned, Leave and Standby. On a phone it sits under the summary card at the top.

Why it works

  • Every name is always in view, so the last engineer sees who is left at a glance.
  • Ticking takes one tap and needs no menu.
  • It sits with the day's numbers, the place people check before sharing.

Watch out

  • With 6 or more engineers on one board the strip wraps onto a second line. It still works, but takes height.

BInside the day's status pill

Where

The pill beside the date, which today says “Draft · Not reviewed”, would read “Draft · 1/3 planned”. Click it for the list. On a phone the list opens from the bottom.

Why it works

  • It takes no new space, and the pill is already where the day's status lives.
  • The pill turns green with “Ready to share” when everyone has ticked.

Watch out

  • The names are hidden until someone clicks, so it is less obvious who is left.
  • The pill is already busy (Draft, Forecast, Locked, Read-only, Holiday), and this adds one more thing to it.

DOn each mission card, next to the engineer

Where

A small “Planned” box beside the engineer's name on every one of their mission cards. Ticking one card ticks all of that engineer's cards. A dot per engineer in the numbers row gives the total.

Why it works

  • You tick right where you just finished planning.
  • Scrolling the board shows which cards are still waiting.

Watch out

  • Adds a control to every card header, which is already dense.
  • One tick on several cards can look like a per-mission tick. It is not.

My pick is A2, “who’s left, by name”. It keeps A’s place in the numbers row, but shows your own tick and only the engineers still missing, instead of every name. That way it fits 9 engineers, or 15, on one line on desktop and in the phone header. The full list is one click away under “All 9”. With 3 or 4 engineers it looks almost the same as A. B is still the fallback if you want nothing new in the numbers row.

Behind the scenes (any option)

  • One small new table: board, date, engineer, ticked by, ticked at. Unticking deletes the row. A short SQL migration adds it with the same access rules as the board.
  • Live sync uses the board's existing real-time updates, so a tick appears for others within a second or two.
  • The /?demo sample board gets the same table, with one engineer already ticked, so the Netlify preview shows it working.

Three questions for you

1. Can others tick for an engineer?My suggestion: you tick your own name, and Admins and managers can also tick for others, with the name of whoever ticked shown in the tooltip.
2. If an engineer changes a mission after ticking, should the tick stay?My suggestion: keep it, so nothing nags people. A small “changed since” dot could come later if it turns out to matter.
3. Should the ticks stay out of the export?My suggestion: yes, on screen only. The crew receives the plan and does not need the check-in.
