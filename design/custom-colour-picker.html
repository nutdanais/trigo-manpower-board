Custom colour picker — proposal

The 16 presets stay exactly as they are. Below them, a Custom section adds a colour square, a hue bar, the hex box and an eyedropper (where the browser supports it). Everything here works — drag, type a hex, or tap a preset.

How it behaves

  • Presets first. Same 16 swatches and tick; one tap still picks and closes, as today.
  • Custom sits under a divider. Dragging the square or hue bar updates the hex and preview live; nothing is saved until Use this colour.
  • Typing a hex moves the square and bar to match. A bad hex gets a red border and is ignored.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move the square (Shift = bigger steps) and the hue bar.
  • Eyedropper shows only in Chrome/Edge (the browser's EyeDropper API); hidden elsewhere.
  • Opens on the current colour, even if it is not a preset, so editing an earlier custom colour starts from it.
  • Same width as today's popover plus ~190px of height; on a phone it stays anchored under the swatch.
  • Also fixes the swatch in the dialog (it showed a tiny dot in a double box); it is now one flat chip, like the Settings tables.
