Present mode · design review · 5 Nov 2026

Show the whole Overview in Present mode, and filter it live

Present plays four slides today. They draw from 3 of the Overview's 13 blocks and none of the five sections that hold the chart filters. This page shows why, what I tried, and one way to fix it. Every screen below is the real app on a sample day.

The gap

Each slide is drawn by hand in present.js, so every new section would be a second copy of code the page already has. Nothing on a slide can be clicked, and the boards are fixed at the start sheet.

The proposal

Keep the four big-type slides. Add the rest by showing the page's own sections, scaled to the screen, in three chapters: Now, In detail, Period. Their buttons keep working, so Group by, Compare, the History tiles and the People views are live. Board chips and a period strip on the stage reach every slide.

You decide

Five choices at the end of this page, each with a recommendation. Nothing in the app has changed yet.

Sample: Thu 5 Nov 2026, 3 boards, 56 people, 12 days of plans in the last 30 days, shown at 1920 × 1080. The stage frame (board chips, period strip, chapter bar) is the proposal, drawn around the real, working sections. Tap any screen to enlarge it.

What I found in the code

F1 · A number on Today has the wrong label

Today reads “Day: 15 missions · Night: 3 missions”. The page's card reads “Day: 7 missions · 15 crew · Night: 3 missions · 3 crew”. Present prints dayMissions, which boardStats defines as a headcount, under the word “missions”. Small fix. I suggest doing it first, apart from this project.

F2 · Slides are drawn by hand

SLIDE_HTML in present.js rebuilds each section in its own markup. That is why Boards shows 3 of 8 columns. Ten more blocks drawn this way is ten more copies that can drift from the page.

F3 · The stage cannot be used

#present-stage covers the page, onKey swallows every key except the slide keys, N, Esc and Tab, and modals are hidden. The page's own buttons are out of reach while presenting.

F4 · The boards are fixed at the start

The start sheet reads the board picker once. To look at one board you stop, change the picker, and start again.

F5 · The Period is invisible

History, People, Engineer workload, Workforce and Boards & areas all follow one date range. A slide of any of them has to say which range it shows, and let you change it.

F6 · Number keys stop at 9

1–9 jump to a slide today. With 11 slides, the last two need another way (the chapter bar, ← →, End).

2 · The chart filters that exist

Every filter is the same kind of control

Every one changes one value and calls render(), and render() already calls Present.redraw(). So a filter clicked on a slide that shows the real section re-draws the slide at once, with no new filter code.

3 · Three ways to cover everything

Draw them, reuse them, or both

A Draw every slide by hand

A new big-type slide for each of the ten missing blocks.

For
  • Best on a wall: 30–40 px type like today's slides.
Against
  • Ten blocks, every control in section 2, six History measures: each needs drawing code and its own click layer.
  • Every figure can drift from the page.
  • The largest build.

B Show the page's real sections

Every slide is the page's own section, scaled to the screen.

For
  • All 13 blocks.
  • Filters are live because the buttons are the page's own.
  • Figures cannot drift. Charts and tooltips come with it.
Against
  • Type is the page's type times 1.1–1.5.
  • Tall views shrink or scroll.
  • Names need hiding in a new place.

C Both Recommended

Keep the four big-type slides as the glance. Add the rest as real sections.

For
  • B's coverage and live filters.
  • A's look on the first four slides, where the room looks first.
  • Any real section can later get a hand-drawn slide without touching the rest.
Against
  • Two looks in one deck.

5 · Using the filters live

A short run, on the real sections

Each frame below is the real Overview section in the real app, after a real click. Only the stage around it is drawn.

1Start

History, Last 30d, all boards, Utilization day by day. The stage header holds the board chips. The period strip, with the range and Compare, shows on every Period slide, so the audience always sees which range they are reading.

2Click “Board Two”

Every slide now shows Board Two: the headline figures, History, People. A Reset filters chip appears. With nothing chosen, “All boards” is on; picking a board from there means only that board; picking another adds it; clearing the last returns to All.

3Click “Week”

The page's own Group by button. The state changes, the page draws again, the stage follows. Nothing new was written for this.

4Click the “Leave / day” tile

Leave by type, by week, for Board Two. This view is tall (chart plus table), so it scales to only 1.08×. See decision 4.

5People follows the chips

Same scope, same range. The table shows ten rows, as the page does; “Show all” is off because it cannot fit. Names show here, so Hide names must reach this slide (see section 6).

6In detail, all boards again

The page's four “In detail” sections fit one slide at 1.46×. I clicked “All boards” to reset the scope.

6 · What to watch

Eight things this design has to get right

Hide names

People, Loans and Host coverage print names, and chart tooltips can too. Each name needs one marker (data-person) that Hide names masks, and a test that fails if a seeded name appears with Hide names on. This is the main risk, so it ships in the first release.

Clicks that leave or edit

A name opens the profile, which can edit a person. Default deny: only controls marked live react; every other click does nothing, and the cursor stays an arrow.

Tall views

The Leave view with its table scales to 1.08×, and People for one board to 1.12×. People with “Show all” would be far taller. Tall slides scroll inside the stage (↑ ↓) and show a “more below” cue.

Tooltips

#chart-tip sits at z-index 60 and the stage at 400, so tooltips would hide behind it. Raised above the stage, a hover tooltip showed in my test.

Filters outlive the meeting

The board choice and the Period boards are saved on the device (manpower.ovBoard, manpower.ovPeriod). A live change would stay for the next person. See decision 3.

Phones

A 1280 px canvas cannot shrink to 400 px. On a phone the real sections show at their own width and scroll. The four big-type slides are unchanged.

Chart height

Line charts draw at a fixed 220 px. In Present they should take the free height; frame 1 shows the unused space under the chart.

Not tested yet

Native date pickers and the engineer dropdown in full screen on the meeting-room PC, a real wall display, phones, other roles, and Thai. These need the real build.

Suggested order

  1. Now, on its own: fix the “missions” label on Today (F1).
  2. Step 1: the stage and the real sections (slides 5–11), board chips, period strip, click rules, Hide names on every section, restore on exit, tests.
  3. Step 2: keys, Reset filters chip, taller charts, more columns on Boards, phone layout.
  4. Only if needed: a hand-drawn slide for any section that is too small from the back of the room.
