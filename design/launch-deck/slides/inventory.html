Inventory & Quality covers both warehouses. Engineers issue consumables and borrow tools through one cart, and approvers decide in the To-Do List. Calibration dates, certificates and customer-part lots are tracked in the same place, and alerts flag low stock and late returns. The figures are the items loaded when the app went live.
