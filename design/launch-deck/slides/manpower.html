The board: day missions, night missions on a midnight-blue fade, leave and standby. On the phone: Overview by engineer.

Manpower Board replaces the whiteboard. Engineers drag people onto missions, leave or standby, and carry over yesterday’s plan in one click. They can forecast weeks ahead and merge the forecast when the day comes. Management sees utilization and idle staff for every board in Overview. Scan the code to open it.
