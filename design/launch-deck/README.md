# Launch deck: Manpower Board and Inventory & Quality

Source of the 7-slide deck (A4 Duo Mist, Sora + Plus Jakarta Sans), published as a claude.ai Slides artifact:
https://claude.ai/artifact/WwAVm7F7ayphpjUC565NHF

- `deck.json`: slide order, sections and fonts
- `slides/*.html`: one file per slide (1920×1080, inline styles), with speaker notes in `<aside>`

Images are uploaded to the artifact (`/_blob/…`); the originals are the screenshots in `design/mobile-parity/` and the Inventory sample-data build.
The design directions it came from are `design/launch-deck-directions.html` and `design/launch-deck-mist-variations.html`.
