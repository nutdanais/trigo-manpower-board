Mission quick panel, moving missions and lending staff

What changes on the board when you click a selected card again, right-click a mission, move a mission to another board, or lend people to another board for a few days. Every menu, dialog and card below is a working copy built from the app's own styles. Click around.

Boards, people, hosts and mission numbers on this page are made-up examples.

What you asked for, and where it is

  1. Click a selected card again to unselect it PR A
  2. Right-click a mission: quick panel PR A
  3. Move or copy a mission to another board PR A with crew: PR B
  4. Extra actions: duplicate, crew, copy as text, host map PR A
  5. Lend people to another board for a day or a few days PR B
1 · Selection

Click a selected card again to unselect it

Today a plain click always selects the card, so the only way out is Clear selection in the side bar. After the change:

  • Click an unselected card: only that card is selected (as now).
  • Click a selected card: it is unselected, also when it is one of several.
  • Ctrl-click and phone taps still add or remove one card at a time.
  • Double-click still opens Edit Employee, and doesn't flip the selection on the way.

3 · Move or copy

Move or copy a mission to another board

Opened from Move or copy to board ›. It changes only the date on screen. Later days carried over from this one follow the new board by themselves.

  • Board: one that is locked or not working that day is greyed out with the reason.
  • Move takes it off this board. Keep a copy leaves this one where it is.
  • Crew: they go back to Standby here, or (PR B) are lent to the other board with the mission.
  • Mission number: keep it or change it. A clash with a mission on the same shift there, hidden ones included, is caught as you type. Try RAYNG.26.0012.
  • Details: keep them, or edit after moving. You land on the other board with the usual Edit Mission form open.
  • Empty day there: pick Amata. Its day has no plan yet, so the dialog offers to carry it over first. Otherwise this one mission would stop carry-over from bringing in the rest of that board's missions.

4 · Extras

Copy as text, for LINE

Copy as text puts this on the clipboard, ready to paste into a chat with the crew or the customer. The phone link stays as plain digits, so it works in any app.

Duplicate, Crew › and Open host location are in the menu above. They reuse things the board already does (the New Mission form, selecting cards, the host's map link), so they look and behave the same as today.

5 · Lending staff PR B

Lend people to another board for a day or a few days

Today "Move to board" on a card moves the person for good. On the board it becomes Lend to board…:

  • Pick the board, From (the date on screen) and Until (the same day unless you change it).
  • That day they leave their home board's mission, and land in the other board's Standby, ready to place.
  • While the loan runs, carry-over keeps them on the borrowing board. The day after it ends they are back home on Standby, with nothing to undo.
  • On a lent card: End loan (back home from the date on screen) and Change loan dates…
  • Moving for good stays in Edit Employee and the Manpower List's own menu, as you said.
  • Not on forecast dates in this round. A forecast still shows people on their home board.
